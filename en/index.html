What is the soldier?

The soldier is a bot in the Yala and Telegram messengers that you can use to play and enjoy Squid Games in your group

The soldier is equipped with a chatbot that you can talk to

Using it, you can talk to the soldier, make requests, and have fun.

The soldier gives gift subscriptions to groups that have just added the robot to the group.

More than 50 users are using the soldier monthly.

  • Holding interesting games
  • Register and log in with your account number
  • See the list of live and deleted players anywhere in the game
  • Talk to the soldier chatbot
  • Speak on behalf of the soldier (suitable for announcing news)
  • And you will have many features with the soldier
